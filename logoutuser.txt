Task 6: Django Logout Operation

Login command:
curl.exe -sS -c cookies.txt -b cookies.txt -X POST "http://127.0.0.1:8000/login/" -H "Content-Type: application/json" --data-binary "@login_body.json"
Login output:
{"message": "Login successful", "username": "demo"}

Logout command:
curl.exe -sS -c cookies.txt -b cookies.txt -X POST "http://127.0.0.1:8000/logout/"
Logout output:
{"message": "Logout successful"}
